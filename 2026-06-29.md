### 📖 Learned
- **From Atomic Habits:**  
  The culture we live in determines which behaviors are attractive to us.  
  This is shaped by three social groups we tend to imitate:  
  1. **The close** (family and friends)  
  2. **The many** (the majority)  
  3. **The powerful** (those with status and influence)  

  To build better habits, we should connect ourselves to a culture where:  
  1. Our desired behavior is their normal behavior  
  2. We already share something in common with that group  

- **From the study techniques video (@KhashayarTalks):**  
  - Rest and doing something you enjoy after studying is important  
  - Environment matters  
  - Understanding the concept is more important than the material itself — if you truly understand it, it stays with you  
  - Highlighting is useful, but don’t fool yourself into thinking “I know this” just because you’ve seen it highlighted — test yourself  
  - Sleep and REM are essential for memory consolidation  
  - Note-taking is important — but using your notes is even more important  
  - A written note is like a map: it shows you the path, but you still have to walk it
