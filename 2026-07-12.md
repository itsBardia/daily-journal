- **The art of starting** — today I truly understood it deeply. Everything feels hard before you begin, but once you take the first step, it becomes simple and natural.  
- This idea connected perfectly with today's chapter from *Atomic Habits* — specifically the **2‑Minute Rule**:  
  - Start small, because starting is more important than the size of the action.  
  - Reduce a habit to less than 2 minutes to remove the friction of beginning.  
  - Examples:  
    - Want to walk? → Just tie your shoes.  
    - Want to write? → Write one sentence.  
    - Want to practice guitar? → Play a few notes.  
- Once you begin, momentum takes over — and the rest becomes easy.
