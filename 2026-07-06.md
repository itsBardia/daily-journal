### 📖 Learned – From Atomic Habits
- **Use the law of least effort.**  
  The human brain naturally prefers the easier option when choosing between tasks.  
- To make good habits stick:  
  - Simplify the actions you need to take.  
  - Align them with your daily routines so they become easier to do.  
- **Reduce friction** for good habits — and **increase friction** for bad ones.  
- Shape your environment to make your desired actions simpler and more automatic.
