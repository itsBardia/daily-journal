# Today — Clarity Through Silence

Over the past week: 2 days of coding, 3 days of math, 1 day of guitar. And I finally finished *Atomic Habits*.

Routine was there too — but I've noticed some problems in my planning. It needs to be tighter, more optimized.

So today, I sat for 20 minutes. No distractions. Just thinking.  
Thinking about what I actually need to do.

And I built a system.

---

## The System: Triangle — Square — Circle

- **Triangle:** The core tasks. Must be done, no excuses.
- **Square:** Tasks that need time-boxing. Allowed, but scheduled.
- **Circle:** Things I must not do. Not now. Not today.

---

### How I applied it:

- **Instagram** → moved to Circle. Too much time wasted there last week.
- **Guitar & NFT trading** → moved to Square. They were distracting me and messing up my planning. Now they get fixed time slots.
- **Daily non-negotiables** → Triangle.  
  Reading, routine (exercise, language, book, small habits) — these shape my future directly.

---

## The Building Analogy

If life is a building:

- **Core tasks** = the skeleton. Structure. Stability.
- **Career & future work** = bricks and blocks. Stacked carefully, one by one.
- **Routine & habits** = the facade. What people see. The finish.
