### 📖 Learned

**From the YouTube video – Language Learning:**

- **Why learn a language?**  
  1. Access to the world (full resources, dictionaries, real content)  
  2. Career opportunities  
  3. Academic opportunities  
  4. Exposure to global ideas — which you can bring back to your own context  

- **What do I lose if I don't learn it?**  
  Opportunities — in every field.

- **Keys to progress:**  
  - Put yourself in the environment + stay curious  
  - Consistency & repetition are meaningless without each other  
  - It's okay to have flaws — but repeating them without fixing is the real problem  
  - Everyone has flaws. Not having flaws is the actual flaw.  
  - A fool repeats the same thing a thousand times and expects different results  

- **How to stay consistent?**  
  1. Start small  
  2. Measure progress  
  3. Master the art of returning (even if you fall off, come back)  
  4. Don't be a perfectionist  
  5. Learn in groups  

- **How to repeat effectively?**  
  1. The 80/20 rule: expose yourself to content where you understand 80% and 20% is new  
  2. Output is critical — input without output leads nowhere  
  3. Get feedback  
  4. Focus on learning cycles (don't just fill time — correct mistakes)  
  5. Document everything

**From Atomic Habits:**

- To build a new habit: **make it attractive**  
  (We're drawn to things that are thoughtfully designed — like flavored snacks — because they feel rewarding)

- When dopamine increases, motivation to act increases  

- It's the **anticipation** of a reward that feels good — not always the reward itself (like a gambler before the bet)

- **Temptation bundling:**  
  After a habit you *want*, do a habit you *need*.  
  Example: After checking Facebook, do some exercise.  
  → Link a necessary habit to a desired one.
